Question 3 (10 marks) Armstrong’s Axioms
Prove the following using Armstrong’s axioms (using only the axioms presented in class). Show all
the steps of your proof, and indicate which of Armstrong’s axioms is applied in each step.
a) (5 marks) Consider the schema R(A, B, C, D, E, F ), and the following functional dependen-
cies: {A → BCD, BC → DE, B → D, D → A}. Show that AF is a superkey.
b) (5 marks) Given the relational schema R(A, B, C, D, E, F ) and the FDs F1: {AB → C,
A → D, CD → EF }. Show that AB → F 

Answer to the Question Number 3(a)

By decomposition, 
A→BCD (given) becomes A→BC and A→D …(i)

By transitivity, 
A→BC (from (i)) and BC→DE (given) implies A→DE …(ii)

From (i) and (ii), by union,
A→BC and A→DE becomes A→BCDE

By augmentation (with F),
A→BCDE becomes AF→BCDEF

Now, compute the closure based on AF→BCDEF. 
AF⁺ = ABCDEF

Since the closure of AF contains all attributes of relation R, it is a superkey.


Answer to the Question Number 3(b)

By augmentation (with B), A→D (given) becomes AB→DB …(i)

By decomposition, AB→DB (from (i)) becomes AB→D and AB→B …(ii)

By union, AB→C (given) and AB→D (from (ii)) implies AB→CD …(iii)

By transitivity, AB→CD (from (iii)) and CD→EF (given) implies AB→EF …(iv)

By decomposition, AB→EF (from (iv)) can be decomposed to AB→E and AB→F …(v)

From (v), AB→F.
